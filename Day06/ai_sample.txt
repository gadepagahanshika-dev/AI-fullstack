AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.
It is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
AI can learn from data, recognize patterns, and make decisions.
It is used in chatbots like ChatGPT, virtual assistants, and customer support systems.
AI powers recommendation systems on platforms such as YouTube, Netflix, and Amazon.
It is used in image and speech recognition, such as face unlock and voice assistants.
AI helps in healthcare by assisting with diagnosis and medical data analysis.
It is used in self-driving vehicles and smart navigation systems.
AI can improve productivity by automating repetitive tasks.
